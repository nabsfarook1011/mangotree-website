Our Story

From the Malabar Coast
to Katong

When The Mango Tree opened in 2001, there was no other restaurant in Singapore doing both South Indian Coastal and North Indian cuisine under one roof. That was the point.

Our recipes come directly from the kitchens of Kerala and Goa — brought over, refined, and kept honest. The spices are fresh. The methods are traditional. The chefs know where they're from.
